# Reference - Ticket priority definitions

| Priority | Means | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | A site cannot ship. Revenue is stopped. | 15 minutes |
| P2 | A team is blocked, a workaround exists. | 1 hour |
| P3 | One person is affected, work continues. | 1 business day |
| P4 | Request or improvement, no one is blocked. | 5 business days |

Priority is set by impact, never by who is asking.
